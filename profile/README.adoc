= 👁️ Wise AI 🦀

Wise AI, formerly Kevin RS, specializes in pushing the boundaries of artificial intelligence by moving beyond language entirely, focusing on the fundamental structure and simulation of the physical world. 

We are the creators of link:https://github.com/wiseaidotdev/lmm[**lmm**] (Large Mathematical Model): the world's first framework written in pure Rust that allows us to encode, predict, and simulate reality itself using mathematical equations.

== The Vision

While LLMs are limited by the symbolic cage of text, Wise AI focuses on **perception, structure, and simulation**. The world is not made of words; it operates on physics, energy, matter, and mathematical structures.

Inspired by the ancient Egyptian **Eye of Horus**: a symbol representing thoughts, intellect, spiritual awareness, and the unification of fragments into cosmic balance, the `lmm` framework strives to fully represent thoughts and physical dynamics using mathematical equations. Just as the Eye of Horus perceives the whole, our LMMs balance multi-modal streams to simulate reality.

== Why Pure Rust?

Rust is the definitive choice for building the next generation of simulation engines and AI tools:

- **Performance**: Physics simulation and mathematical modeling demand blazingly fast execution.
- **Memory Safety**: Rust's ownership model avoids entire classes of bugs, making systems radically more reliable.
- **Zero-Cost Abstractions**: Allowing us to encode complex mathematical constraints seamlessly.

== Core Project: LMM

The `lmm` framework is a category shift in intelligence architecture:

- **Mathematical Intelligence**: Focuses on equation discovery and symbolic regression, learning compact mathematical rules directly from multi-modal data.
- **Simulation is Core**: Moves beyond describing the world to computing and simulating its future directly.
- **Multimodal By Default**: Processes text, images, video, and numerical structures synchronously to model deep physical reality.

== AutoGPT

We are the original creators of the link:https://github.com/wiseaidotdev/autogpt[autogpt] Rust framework. While our primary focus is now on building intelligence without LLMs through our `lmm` project, we continue to actively maintain the AutoGPT framework for the open-source community.
